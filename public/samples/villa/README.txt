PHONIEX sample villa — test files
=================================

An illustrative sample made by PHONIEX for testing the website. Not a real property.

Property
- Land: 30 m x 40 m plot (1,200 m²), boundary wall with sliding gate and pedestrian gate
- Villa: 2 floors, 5 bedrooms (incl. guest bedroom), majlis, family room, maid's room
- Ground floor: 234 m² · First floor: 154 m²
- Outside: swimming pool with deck and shade, carport, majlis annex, front lawn, back garden, palm trees

Files
- villa-sample.glb ........ 3D model (glTF binary). Upload under "3D design model".
- floor-plan-ground.json .. Ground floor plan. Import under "Floor plan diagram" -> "Import plan file".
- floor-plan-first.json ... First floor plan (same import button).

Units are metres. The model's front gate faces the street.
